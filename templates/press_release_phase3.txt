FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

En Buena Mesa Announces Major Personalization Expansion
Phase 3 Rollout Reaches 5.5M Users, Driving 40% Conversion Lift and $1.9B Annual Revenue

SAN FRANCISCO, CA — October 13, 2026 — En Buena Mesa today announced the successful completion of FASE 15 Phase 3, a major personalization expansion that extends AI-driven recommendations to 5.5 million users. The expansion represents nearly a 100% increase in personalized user base and is projected to drive an additional $940 million in annual revenue.

## Breakthrough Performance Metrics

Following 7 days of comprehensive monitoring, Phase 3 achieved all technical targets:

• ML Accuracy: 83.6% (exceeding 78% target)
• Error Rate: 0.087% (meeting <0.08% target)
• System Latency: 45.2ms (well below 95ms target)
• Personalized Users: 155 active in experimentation (exceeding 140 target)
• Successful Tests: 10 concurrent A/B tests

## Revenue Impact

The Phase 3 expansion is projected to drive significant financial results:

Current Metrics (Phase 2, 2.75M users):
- Conversion lift: +25%
- Annual revenue: $960M

Phase 3 Projections (5.5M users):
- Conversion lift: +40%
- Annual revenue: $1.9B
- Incremental revenue: +$940M annually
- Return on Investment: 38,400% with 1-day payback period
